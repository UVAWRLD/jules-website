In detail

44LLC, NEW YORK

Three roles · 2024-2026 · New York and remote

The company:

44LLC is a strategy and design studio in Brooklyn. It works with hotel groups, hospitality brands and private clients, from the first positioning of a project to the standards that architects and operators build from. Clients include Jumeirah and Rosewood.

Strategy Consultant · 2026 · Remote

I worked remotely from France on positioning, innovation strategy and new-business pitches for hotel groups and brands. I also advised the studio on where to spend its time across projects and saw the Jumeirah standards through to final delivery.

Product Manager · Sep 2025-Feb 2026

Jumeirah’s design and product standards cover hotels and resorts, branded residences and branded villas, and I ran the programme like a product. Requirements came in from the client, chapters went out one release at a time, and review rounds ran across three time zones. To keep that pace, I built our own production tools: scripts that turn structured text into InDesign-ready layouts, batch processing for review documents, and interactive boards for client reviews. In parallel I shaped product strategy for a European tile manufacturer deciding how to split its range between trade and retail customers.

Strategist · 2024-2025

Most of my first year went into pitches. I built brand strategy proposals for hospitality groups and private clients, taking each from first positioning to final presentation, and wrote the brand-led concepts behind Jumeirah’s residential and villa standards.

Focus: Positioning · Pitches · Product management · Production tooling

NYC INNOVATION COLLECTIVE, NEW YORK

Associate · 2024-2025 · New York

The company:

A nonprofit alliance of New York tech organisations. Its members are accelerators, incubators and other startup programmes, and it works to make the city’s startup scene better connected and easier to get into.

The role:

I started with the numbers. After cleaning up the membership records, I segmented members by stage and sector so we knew who to approach for which events and partnerships. That work shaped the website redesign, which I built around the two things people came for: joining and attending. I also ran the events themselves, mostly founder meetups and panel evenings.

Focus: Data analysis · UX/UI · Community · Events

VYBE TOGETHER, NEW YORK

Creative Director & Co-Founder · 2020-2024 · New York

The company:

An app for finding and hosting small get-togethers nearby. A host posts a gathering, guests apply, and the host decides who comes.

The role:

I co-founded Vybe and led its brand, go-to-market and app design, working with our product and engineering team from first concept to launch. In 2020 our first marketing video went much further than the product had. It reached national press before we had users, and we took it down. That taught me how fast a brand’s tone can run ahead of what it’s selling, and it also showed how far one small team’s idea could travel. When we came back, the brand was built around what the app had always been for: small gatherings, with the host choosing the guests.

Focus: Brand · Go-to-market · App design · Founding team

JUICE HOUSE, NEW YORK
